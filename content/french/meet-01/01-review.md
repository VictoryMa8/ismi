# Greet someone and say goodbye

Unreviewed private F25 draft · french-meet-01-01 · No publication approval, rights acceptance or model audio.

Alex meets a familiar friend Camille in the daytime, then leaves.

Goal: Open and close a short meeting in the stated context.

## Dialogue

- **Alex:** Bonjour. · Hello.
- **Camille:** Salut ! · Hi!
- **Alex:** Au revoir. · Goodbye.
- **Camille:** Au revoir. · Goodbye.

## Teaching and spelling help

### Open a meeting

Bonjour. · Hello.

A daytime greeting usable beyond familiar friends; choose the greeting for the stated situation.

Recall before reveal: Greet someone during the day.

### Greet a familiar friend

Salut ! · Hi!

Use this casual greeting in the explicitly familiar-friend role here; it is not a rule for every new acquaintance.

Recall before reveal: Greet a familiar friend casually.

### Close an exchange

Au revoir. · Goodbye.

This closes the meeting rather than introducing a new person.

Recall before reveal: Say goodbye when leaving.
