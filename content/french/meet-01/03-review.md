# Meet someone politely

Unreviewed private F25 draft · french-meet-01-03 · No publication approval, rights acceptance or model audio.

Alex and Camille meet as new adult acquaintances. The exercise explicitly keeps vous.

Goal: Keep polite singular address through a name exchange.

## Dialogue

- **Alex:** Bonjour. · Hello.
- **Alex:** Comment vous appelez-vous ? · What is your name? (polite singular here)
- **Camille:** Je m’appelle Camille. · My name is Camille.
- **Camille:** Et vous ? · And you? (polite singular here)
- **Alex:** Je m’appelle Alex. · My name is Alex.
- **Camille:** Au revoir. · Goodbye.

## Teaching and spelling help

### Ask politely

Comment vous appelez-vous ? · What is your name? (polite singular here)

Here vous addresses one person politely. It can also address several people; this scene explicitly has one addressee.
