## Teaching and spelling help

### Signal a missed message

Je n’ai pas compris. · I did not understand.

Learn this as a whole repair expression for now; this unit does not assess past-tense conjugation.

Recall before reveal: Say that you did not understand.

### Ask a friend to repeat

Tu peux répéter, s’il te plaît ? · Can you repeat, please? (familiar)

Request the words again when you missed them; this does not specifically ask for slower delivery. This composed repair phrase needs fluent confirmation.

Recall before reveal: Ask a familiar partner for the words again.

### Ask for a slower pace

Tu peux parler plus lentement, s’il te plaît ? · Can you speak more slowly, please? (familiar)

Ask for a change of pace when the words are too fast. This is distinct from simply requesting another repetition. This composed phrase needs fluent confirmation.

Recall before reveal: Ask a familiar partner specifically to slow down.

### Acknowledge help

Merci. · Thank you.

Thank the person after clarification; this does not supply your name or ask a question.

Recall before reveal: Thank someone who helped you.
