# Greet someone

Unreviewed F26 candidate · khmer-meet-01-01 · text-only development-preview exception authorized separately; no linguistic approval or model audio.

Your partner introduces you to a friend of a similar age at their home. Greet each other, introduce yourselves, then thank the host for welcoming you.

Goal: Greet a peer and thank them for their welcome.

## Dialogue

- **Host:** សួស្ដី · sua-sdey · Hello.
- **Guest:** សួស្ដី · sua-sdey · Hello.
- **Host:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Sam។ · khnhom chhmoh Sam · My name is Sam.
- **Guest:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Alex។ · khnhom chhmoh Alex · My name is Alex.
- **Guest:** អរគុណ · aw-kun · Thank you.

## Teaching and optional script inventory

### Greet a peer

សួស្ដី

sua-sdey · Hello.

Use this candidate for a casual meeting between adults of similar age. Review the greeting and setting together; this is not a universal address rule.

Recall: You meet a friend of your partner. Offer a casual hello.

### Thank someone

អរគុណ

aw-kun · Thank you.

Use this after someone helps you. It acknowledges help rather than asking for new information.

Recall: Someone repeats their name for you. Thank them.

### Get someone’s attention

សូមទោស

som-toh · Excuse me / sorry.

This candidate marks an apology or excuse me. Review the situation and intonation before recording it; it does not itself ask for repetition.

Recall: Politely get someone’s attention before asking for help.

### Recognize a formal greeting

ជំរាបសួរ

jom-riab-sua · Hello (formal greeting).

Keep the formal greeting distinct from the casual scene. A fluent reviewer must confirm when it fits; recognition here is not a cultural etiquette assessment.

Recall: You need to recognize the formal greeting from this lesson.
