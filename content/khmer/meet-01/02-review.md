# Introduce yourself

Unreviewed F26 candidate · khmer-meet-01-02 · no publication approval or model audio.

You are Alex, meeting Sam through a friend. You are both adults of a similar age. Use the assigned names in this practice.

Goal: Give your name and ask someone else’s name.

## Dialogue

- **Alex:** សួស្ដី · sua-sdey · Hello.
- **Sam:** សួស្ដី · sua-sdey · Hello.
- **Alex:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Alex។ · khnhom chhmoh Alex · My name is Alex.
- **Alex:** អ្នកឈ្មោះអី? · neak chhmoh ey? · What is your name?
- **Sam:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Sam។ · khnhom chhmoh Sam · My name is Sam.
- **Alex:** អរគុណ · aw-kun · Thank you.

### Ask a name

អ្នកឈ្មោះអី?

neak chhmoh ey? · What is your name?

The draft scene uses adults of similar age. The address choice needs fluent review; do not generalize it to every relationship or formal meeting.

Recall: Ask the person you just met for their name.
