# Get to know each other

Unreviewed F26 candidate · khmer-meet-01-03 · no publication approval or model audio.

At a small gathering, Alex is from America and Sam is from Phnom Penh. These are assigned practice details, not assumptions about you.

Goal: Exchange a name and one simple personal detail.

## Dialogue

- **Alex:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Alex។ · khnhom chhmoh Alex · My name is Alex.
- **Alex:** ខ្ញុំមកពីអាមេរិក។ · khnhom mok pii aa-me-rik · I am from America.
- **Alex:** អ្នកមកពីណា? · neak mok pii naa? · Where are you from?
- **Sam:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Sam។ · khnhom chhmoh Sam · My name is Sam.
- **Sam:** ខ្ញុំមកពីភ្នំពេញ។ · khnhom mok pii Phnom Penh · I am from Phnom Penh.
- **Alex:** អរគុណ · aw-kun · Thank you.

## Teaching and optional script inventory

### Ask where someone is from

អ្នកមកពីណា?

This asks about origin in the draft scene. It is a different request from asking for someone’s name. The colloquial wording needs contemporary review.
