# Keep the exchange going

Unreviewed F26 candidate · khmer-meet-01-05 · text-only development-preview exception authorized separately; no linguistic approval or model audio.

A different gathering: you are Sam and meet Alex at a friend’s home. Use earlier phrases in a changed role and clarify when needed.

Goal: Greet someone, exchange details and repair a missed question.

## Dialogue

- **Sam:** សួស្ដី · sua-sdey · Hello.
- **Sam:** ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Sam។ · khnhom chhmoh Sam · My name is Sam.
- **Alex:** អ្នកឈ្មោះអី? · neak chhmoh ey? · What is your name?
- **Sam:** សូមនិយាយម្តងទៀត។ · som niyeay mdong tiet · Please say it again.

## Teaching and optional script inventory

### Greet a peer

សួស្ដី

sua-sdey · Hello.

Use this candidate for a casual meeting between adults of similar age. Review the greeting and setting together; this is not a universal address rule.

Recall: You meet a friend of your partner. Offer a casual hello.

### Recognize another name

ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ Sam។

khnhom chhmoh Sam · My name is Sam.

Sam is a different practice name. Keep the name asked for in view; both name sentences are possible in other situations.

Recall: You are playing Sam. Introduce yourself.

### Ask where someone is from

អ្នកមកពីណា?

neak mok pii naa? · Where are you from?

This asks about origin in the draft scene. It is a different request from asking for someone’s name. The colloquial wording needs contemporary review.

Recall: Ask where your new acquaintance is from.

### Ask to hear it again

សូមនិយាយម្តងទៀត។

som niyeay mdong tiet · Please say it again.

Use the repetition request when you missed the words. It is distinct from a request to change speed. The script/help is an original modern candidate informed by a historical pattern, pending fluent review.

Recall: You missed a name. Ask the person to say it again.

### Ask for a slower exchange

សូមនិយាយយឺតៗ។

som niyeay yeut yeut · Please speak slowly.

Use the speed request when the words came too quickly. This modern script/help candidate needs fluent review; the historical reference does not approve it.

Recall: You heard the words but the pace was too fast. Ask for slower speech.
