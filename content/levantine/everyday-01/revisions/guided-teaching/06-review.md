# 6. What are we doing tomorrow? — guided teaching draft

Unpublished AI-assisted revision for owner review. The original packages and all prior versions remain intact. Stable lesson/step IDs and eight deterministic grading targets are preserved.

Goal: Suggest a shared visit, ask for a time, and confirm tomorrow’s plan.

## Open tomorrow’s shared plan

شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ · Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra?

What will we do tomorrow?

Raḥ points forward; niʿmal is we do or make. Bukra supplies tomorrow, so this is a shared future plan.

Recall: Ask Lina what the two of you will do tomorrow.

شو (shū) = what | رح (raḥ) = future action | نعمل (niʿmal) = we do / make | بكرا (bukra) = tomorrow

## Add who and when

نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟ · Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl?

Shall we visit my family after work?

Ahli names your own family. Baʿd ish-shughl puts the visit after work; baʿdēn by itself means then.

Recall: You are Omar. Suggest visiting your family after work.

نزور (nzūr) = we visit | أهلي (ahli) = my family | بعد الشغل (baʿd ish-shughl) = after work

## Ask for the missing detail

تمام. أي ساعة؟ · Tamām. Ayy sāʿa?

Okay. What time?

Tamām accepts the idea. Ayy sāʿa asks for the time, completing the arrangement.

Recall: Accept the visit idea, then ask what time.

تمام (tamām) = okay | أي ساعة (ayy sāʿa) = what time

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, internal:ismi/everyday-01/06/guided-teaching

## Give the first time

الساعة ستة. · Is-sāʿa sitte.

At six o’clock.

Is-sāʿa introduces the clock time. The dialogue shares an evening context; in a real plan, clarify morning or evening if needed.

Recall: Omar asks what time. Say six o’clock.

الساعة (is-sāʿa) = at / the hour | ستة (sitte) = six

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, internal:ismi/everyday-01/06/guided-teaching

## Change one piece of the plan

الساعة سبعة. · Is-sāʿa sabʿa.

At seven o’clock.

Keep the time frame and change just the number. Sabʿa is seven; sitte is six.

Recall: In a new arrangement, the time is seven instead. Give the new time.

الساعة (is-sāʿa) = at / the hour | سبعة (sabʿa) = seven

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, internal:ismi/everyday-01/06/guided-teaching

## Confirm the whole new arrangement

آه، بكرا الساعة سبعة نشرب شاي سوا. · Āh, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nishrab shāy sawa.

Yes, tomorrow at seven we’ll drink tea together.

Combine the day, time, and activity. Sawa keeps the tea plan shared; the tomorrow context makes its future setting clear.

Recall: Accept tomorrow at seven and confirm drinking tea together.

آه (āh) = yes | بكرا (bukra) = tomorrow | الساعة سبعة (is-sāʿa sabʿa) = at seven | نشرب شاي (nishrab shāy) = we drink tea | سوا (sawa) = together
