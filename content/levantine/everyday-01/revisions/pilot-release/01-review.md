# 1. Check in with someone you love — release review

Unapproved October 3 candidate. Exact package: [01-lesson.json](01-lesson.json).

## Dialogue

**Fattoush → Knafeh**

شو عامل؟

Shū ʿāmel?

How are you doing? (casual check-in to Knafeh)

**Knafeh → Fattoush**

أنا منيح. وإنتِ؟

Ana mnīḥ. W-inti?

I’m well. And you? (to Fattoush)

**Fattoush**

أنا تعبانة شوي.

Ana taʿbāne shwayy.

I’m a little tired. (Fattoush speaking)

**Knafeh**

أنا تعبان كمان.

Ana taʿbān kamān.

I’m tired too. (Knafeh speaking)

**Fattoush → Knafeh**

بدك ترتاح؟

Biddak tirtāḥ?

Do you want to rest? (to Knafeh)

**Knafeh**

آه، بدي أرتاح شوي.

Āh, biddi artāḥ shwayy.

Yes, I want to rest a little.

Feedback: Ana mnīḥ answers about Knafeh himself. The lesson’s question is a casual check-in.

Retry hint: Use ana and Knafeh’s form of “well.”
