# 4. Let's eat together — release review

Unapproved October 3 candidate. Exact package: [04-lesson.json](04-lesson.json).

## Dialogue

**Knafeh**

أنا جوعان.

Ana jūʿān.

I’m hungry. (Knafeh speaking)

**Fattoush → Knafeh**

أنا جوعانة كمان. شو رأيك ناكل سوا؟

Ana jūʿāne kamān. Shū raʾyak nākol sawa?

I’m hungry too. What do you think about eating together?

**Knafeh**

نعمل رز وسلطة؟

Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa?

Shall we make rice and salad?

**Fattoush**

آه، خلّينا نعمل رز وسلطة.

Āh, khallīna niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa.

Yes, let’s make rice and salad.

**Knafeh**

بدك شاي مع الأكل؟

Biddik shāy maʿ il-akel?

Do you want tea with the food?

**Fattoush**

آه، بدون سكر.

Āh, bidūn sukkar.

Yes, without sugar.

- b: أنا مش جوعان هلّق. · Ana mish jūʿān hallaʾ. · I’m not hungry now. (Knafeh speaking). Rationale: Mish reverses the meaning and says Knafeh is not hungry.
- c (accepted): أنا جوعان. · Ana jūʿān. · I’m hungry. (Knafeh speaking). Rationale: Jūʿān is the masculine description Knafeh uses here.

Feedback: Jūʿān is the masculine description Knafeh uses here.

Retry hint: The speaker is Knafeh, and the meaning is positive.

### e04-03

- b (accepted): شو رأيك ناكل سوا؟ · Shū raʾyak nākol sawa? · What do you think about eating together? (to Knafeh). Rationale: This proposes a shared action and addresses Knafeh with raʾyak.

Feedback: This proposes a shared action and addresses Knafeh with raʾyak.

Retry hint: The invitation needs sawa, “together.”

### e04-04

Instruction: The agreed idea is rice AND salad. Suggest both foods.

Roles: {}

Prompt: شو نعمل للأكل؟ · Shū niʿmal lal-akel? · What shall we make to eat?

- a (accepted): نعمل رز وسلطة؟ · Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? · Shall we make rice and salad?. Rationale: W joins the two dishes as one meal.
- b: نعمل رز ولا سلطة؟ · Niʿmal ruzz walla salaṭa? · Shall we make rice or salad?. Rationale: Walla asks the listener to choose one option; the requested plan includes both.
- c: نعمل شاي؟ · Niʿmal shāy? · Shall we make tea?. Rationale: Tea is a drink and does not name the two agreed dishes.

Feedback: W joins the two dishes as one meal.

Retry hint: Join the foods with “and,” not “or.”

Retry hint: Agree and keep both dishes in the plan.

- b (accepted): أنا مش جوعان هلّق. · Ana mish jūʿān hallaʾ. · I’m not hungry now. (Knafeh speaking). Rationale: Mish negates the description jūʿān; hallaʾ limits it to now.

Retry hint: Use the negative description, not a different feeling.

### e04-08

Instruction: Closing exchange, part 2: the meal is agreed. Accept tea without sugar.

Roles: {"speakerId": "knafeh", "addresseeId": "fattoush", "responseSpeakerId": "fattoush", "responseAddresseeId": "knafeh"}

- c (accepted): آه، بدون سكر، شكراً. · Āh, bidūn sukkar, shukran. · Yes, without sugar, thank you.. Rationale: This reuses the drink language to finish the meal arrangement.

Feedback: This reuses the drink language to finish the meal arrangement.

Retry hint: Accept the offer and specify without sugar.
