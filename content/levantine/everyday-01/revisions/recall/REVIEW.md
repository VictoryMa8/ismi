## rest-a-little

Required concepts: yes, want to rest, a little.

1. After a long walk, Fattoush offers you a break. Accept and say you want to rest a little.
2. You have finished helping at home. Your friend suggests a break. Accept and say you want to rest a little. **Reserved for a later due day.**

Accepted models:

- **آه، بدي أرتاح شوي.** — Āh, biddi artāḥ shwayy. — Yes, I want to rest a little.
  Rationale: Accepting a break and stating your wish to rest a little.
  Authored ASCII alternatives: ah biddi artah shwayy, ah biddi artah shway.

Known contextual error:

- **بدك ترتاح؟** — Biddak tirtāḥ?. This asks whether the other person wants to rest; the situation asks you to accept for yourself.

Other answers receive “Unable to judge,” model comparison and an optional report.

## request-tea

Required concepts: request, tea.

1. You are visiting a friend. Knafeh is making drinks. Ask him for tea.
2. At the end of a meal, Knafeh offers to bring a drink. Ask him for tea. **Reserved for a later due day.**

Accepted models:

- **شاي، لو سمحت.** — Shāy, law samaḥt. — Tea, please. (to Knafeh)
  Rationale: Both the polite tea request and the taught “I want tea” state the requested drink.
  Authored ASCII alternatives: shay law samaht.
- **بدي شاي.** — Biddi shāy. — I want tea.
  Rationale: Both the polite tea request and the taught “I want tea” state the requested drink.
  Authored ASCII alternatives: biddi shay.

Known contextual error:

- **قهوة، لو سمحتي.** — ʾAhwe, law samaḥti.. This requests coffee, while the situation asks for tea.

Other answers receive “Unable to judge,” model comparison and an optional report.

## confirm-seven

Required concepts: time, seven.

1. A friend suggests meeting at six, but you agreed on seven. State the correct time: at seven.
2. Someone asks when to arrive for your visit. The arranged time is seven. State the time: at seven. **Reserved for a later due day.**

Accepted models:

- **الساعة سبعة.** — Is-sāʿa sabʿa. — At seven o’clock.
  Rationale: The taught time phrase specifies seven o’clock.
  Authored ASCII alternatives: is-saa sab'a, is saa saba, is-saa sab3a.

Known contextual error:

- **الساعة ستة.** — Is-sāʿa sitte.. This gives six o’clock, while the agreed time is seven.

Other answers receive “Unable to judge,” model comparison and an optional report.
