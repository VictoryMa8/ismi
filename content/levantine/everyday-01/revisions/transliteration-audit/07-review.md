## Dialogue

**Knafeh → Fattoush**

شو عاملة؟

Shū ʿāmle?

How are you doing? (casual check-in to Fattoush)

**Fattoush**

أنا منيحة. زرت أمي وبعدين ارتحت.

Ana mnīḥa. Zurt immi w-baʿdēn irtaḥt.

I’m well. I visited my mother and then rested.

**Knafeh**

وكيفها؟

W-kīfha?

And how is she?

**Fattoush → Knafeh**

منيحة. شو رأيك ناكل سوا بكرا؟

Mnīḥa. Shū raʾyak nākol sawa bukra?

She’s well. What do you think about eating together tomorrow?

**Knafeh**

تمام. نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة؟

Tamām. Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa?

Okay. Shall we make rice and salad at seven?

**Fattoush**

آه، وبعدين نشرب شاي.

Āh, w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy.

Yes, and then we’ll drink tea.

## Teaching cards

### Connect your condition and your story

أنا منيحة. زرت أمي وبعدين ارتحت.

Ana mnīḥa. Zurt immi w-baʿdēn irtaḥt.

I’m well. I visited my mother and then rested.

Fattoush answers about herself first, then gives two completed activities. W-baʿdēn preserves visit first, rest second.

Recall: You are Fattoush. Say you’re well, then report visiting your mother and resting, in that order.

- أنا منيحة · ana mnīḥa · I’m well · Fattoush
- زرت أمي · zurt immi · I visited my mother
- وبعدين ارتحت · w-baʿdēn irtaḥt · and then I rested

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, https://media.lingualism.com/wp-content/uploads/Sample-Palestinian-Arabic-Verbs-Lingualism.pdf, internal:ismi/review-cases/LEV-2026-09-30-01, https://files.lingualism.com/wp-content/uploads/SAMPLE-Levantine-Arabic-Verbs-Lingualism.pdf, internal:ismi/everyday-01/07/guided-teaching

### Track who the question is about

وكيفها؟

W-kīfha?

And how is she?

After the mother is mentioned, -ha points to her. The reply mnīḥa now describes the mother, rather than Fattoush.

Recall: Fattoush tells you she visited her mother. Ask how her mother is.

- وكيفها · w-kīfha · and how is she · mother

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, https://media.lingualism.com/wp-content/uploads/Sample-Palestinian-Arabic-Verbs-Lingualism.pdf, https://resources.lingualism.com/levantine-arabic/family-3/, internal:ismi/everyday-01/07/guided-teaching

### Move the conversation to tomorrow

شو رأيك ناكل سوا بكرا؟

Shū raʾyak nākol sawa bukra?

What do you think about eating together tomorrow? (to Knafeh)

Reuse the shared-meal invitation and add bukra. The address raʾyak is to Knafeh.

Recall: You are Fattoush. Ask Knafeh about eating together tomorrow.

- شو رأيك · shū raʾyak · what do you think · to Knafeh
- ناكل سوا · nākol sawa · we eat together
- بكرا · bukra · tomorrow

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, https://media.lingualism.com/wp-content/uploads/Sample-Palestinian-Arabic-Verbs-Lingualism.pdf, https://resources.lingualism.com/levantine-arabic/food-and-drink-3/, internal:ismi/everyday-01/07/guided-teaching

### Attach a time to the meal

نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة.

Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa.

We’ll make rice and salad at seven.

Keep both foods and the new time. You are confirming rice and salad at seven, rather than changing the meal.

Recall: Confirm rice and salad at seven.

- نعمل · niʿmal · we make
- رز وسلطة · ruzz w-salaṭa · rice and salad
- الساعة سبعة · is-sāʿa sabʿa · at seven

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, https://resources.lingualism.com/levantine-arabic/verbs-3/, https://resources.lingualism.com/levantine-arabic/food-and-drink-3/, internal:ismi/everyday-01/07/guided-teaching

### Retell a different day

اشتغلت وبعدين ارتحت.

Ishtaghalt w-baʿdēn irtaḥt.

I worked and then rested.

Use familiar pieces with a changed activity. Start with work, then rest; do not carry the earlier mother-visit story into this exchange.

Recall: In a new conversation, say you worked and then rested.

- اشتغلت · ishtaghalt · I worked
- وبعدين · w-baʿdēn · and then
- ارتحت · irtaḥt · I rested

Sources: https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/palestine-lexicon/lexicon, https://media.lingualism.com/wp-content/uploads/Sample-Palestinian-Arabic-Verbs-Lingualism.pdf, internal:ismi/everyday-01/07/guided-teaching

### Close with a connected plan

تمام، بكرا الساعة سبعة. وبعدين نشرب شاي.

Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa. W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy.

Okay, tomorrow at seven. And afterwards we’ll drink tea.

Confirm the day and time, then add what follows. The prompt has already established the meal, so the reply can leave that shared detail understood.

Recall: Accept tomorrow at seven, then add drinking tea afterwards.

- تمام · tamām · okay
- بكرا الساعة سبعة · bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa · tomorrow at seven
- وبعدين · w-baʿdēn · and then
- نشرب شاي · nishrab shāy · we drink tea

### e07-01

Fattoush is well. She visited her mother and then rested. Respond with her account.

Roles: {"speakerId": "knafeh", "addresseeId": "fattoush", "responseSpeakerId": "fattoush", "responseAddresseeId": "knafeh"}

- b (accepted): أنا منيحة. زرت أمي وبعدين ارتحت. · Ana mnīḥa. Zurt immi w-baʿdēn irtaḥt. · I’m well. I visited my mother and then rested.. Rationale: This preserves Fattoush’s self-description and the visit-before-rest order.

### e07-02

Knafeh’s follow-up refers to Fattoush’s mother. Answer about her, not yourself.

Roles: {"speakerId": "knafeh", "addresseeId": "fattoush", "responseSpeakerId": "fattoush", "responseAddresseeId": "knafeh"}

Feedback: The mother is the most recently named woman. The answer stays about her.

Retry: Keep the person referred to by -ha.
