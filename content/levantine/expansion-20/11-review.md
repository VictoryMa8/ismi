# 11 · Find something at home

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Finding the key before leaving; the bag is a plausible but wrong location.

Teaching note: Use wēn for location, ʿa for on, and janb for beside. The negative corrects the proposed location, not the usefulness of the bag.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: وين المفتاح؟ — Wēn il-miftāḥ? — Where is the key?
- Knafeh: المفتاح عالطاولة. — Il-miftāḥ ʿa-ṭ-ṭāwle. — The key is on the table.
- Fattoush: مش بالشنطة؟ — Mish bish-shanṭa? — Isn't it in the bag?
- Knafeh: لا، جنب الكتاب. — Laʾ, janb il-ktāb. — No, beside the book.
- Fattoush: آه، لقيته عالطاولة. — Āh, laʾētu ʿa-ṭ-ṭāwle. — Ah, I found it on the table.
- Knafeh: يلا نطلع سوا. — Yalla niṭlaʿ sawa. — Come on, let's go out together.
