# 12 · Ask for a little help

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Opening a window at home; asking for help is different from saying where an object is.

Teaching note: Mumkin makes a request. Tiftaḥ addresses a male listener; biddik in the reply addresses Fattoush. Open-window language is contextual, not a universal instruction.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: ممكن تساعدني شوي؟ — Mumkin tsāʿidni shway? — Could you help me a little?
- Knafeh: أكيد، شو بدك؟ — Akīd, shū biddik? — Of course, what do you need?
- Fattoush: ممكن تفتح الشباك شوي؟ — Mumkin tiftaḥ ish-shubbāk shway? — Could you open the window a little?
- Knafeh: آه، الشباك مسكر. — Āh, ish-shubbāk msakkar. — Yes, the window is closed.
- Fattoush: شكرا، هيك أحسن. — Shukran, hēk aḥsan. — Thanks, that's better.
- Knafeh: بدك إشي تاني؟ — Biddik ishi tāni? — Do you need anything else?
