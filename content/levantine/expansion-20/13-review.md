# 13 · Share household tasks

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Sharing the dishes and table after a meal; speakers keep their own tasks distinct.

Teaching note: Ana and inta preserve who does each task. Nimsaḥ and nighsil are shared suggestions; the first-person forms describe the agreed division.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: خلينا نرتب المطبخ. — Khallīna nrattib il-maṭbakh. — Let's tidy the kitchen.
- Knafeh: أنا بغسل الصحون. — Ana baghsil iṣ-ṣuḥūn. — I'll wash the dishes.
- Fattoush: وأنا بمسح الطاولة. — W-ana bamsaḥ iṭ-ṭāwle. — And I'll wipe the table.
- Knafeh: وين الصابون؟ — Wēn iṣ-ṣābūn? — Where is the soap?
- Fattoush: جنب المجلى. — Janb il-majla. — Beside the sink.
- Knafeh: تمام، وبعدين بنرتاح. — Tamām, w-baʿdēn bnirtāḥ. — Okay, and afterwards we'll rest.
