# 14 · Say what you need

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Choosing rest and water after work; a need is different from an invitation to go out.

Teaching note: Biddi states a want/need. Mish hal-laʾ declines the current timing while leaving another time possible. Do not interpret every refusal as a permanent dislike.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: شو بدك بعد الشغل؟ — Shū biddak baʿd ish-shughl? — What would you like after work?
- Knafeh: بدي أرتاح شوي. — Biddi artāḥ shway. — I want to rest a little.
- Fattoush: بدك مي؟ — Biddak mayy? — Would you like water?
- Knafeh: آه، مي باردة. — Āh, mayy bārde. — Yes, cold water.
- Fattoush: نطلع هلأ؟ — Niṭlaʿ hallaʾ? — Shall we go out now?
- Knafeh: مش هلأ، بعد شوي. — Mish hallaʾ, baʿd shway. — Not now, in a little while.
