# 15 · Agree on an evening at home

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Agreeing to stay home, cook and have tea in that order.

Teaching note: Bil-bēt names being at home. Khallīna proposes a shared action; aw-wal is first and baʿdēn is afterwards. The accepted plan includes both order and time.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: نضل بالبيت الليلة؟ — Nḍall bil-bēt il-lēle? — Shall we stay home tonight?
- Knafeh: آه، خلينا نطبخ سوا. — Āh, khallīna nuṭbukh sawa. — Yes, let's cook together.
- Fattoush: نعمل رز وسلطة؟ — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? — Shall we make rice and salad?
- Knafeh: تمام، الساعة سبعة. — Tamām, is-sāʿa sabʿa. — Okay, at seven.
- Fattoush: وبعدين نشرب شاي. — W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy. — And afterwards we'll drink tea.
- Knafeh: يعني أول إشي الأكل، وبعدين الشاي. — Yaʿni awwal ishi il-akil, w-baʿdēn ish-shāy. — So food first, and then tea.
