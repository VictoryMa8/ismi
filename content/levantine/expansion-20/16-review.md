# 16 · Introduce someone close

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Introducing a sister in a family photograph without assigning that identity to either guide.

Teaching note: Hāy points to a feminine referent. Ukhti is my sister; ukhtak would be your sister to a male listener. The guides discuss a photograph, not a third illustrated speaker.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: مين هاي بالصورة؟ — Mīn hāy biṣ-ṣūra? — Who is this in the picture?
- Knafeh: هاي أختي سارة. — Hāy ukhti Sāra. — This is my sister Sara.
- Fattoush: هي ساكنة هون؟ — Hiyye sākne hōn? — Does she live here?
- Knafeh: لا، ساكنة برام الله. — Laʾ, sākne b-Rāmalla. — No, she lives in Ramallah.
- Fattoush: بتزوركم كتير؟ — Bitzūrkum ktīr? — Does she visit you often?
- Knafeh: آه، بتزورنا كل أسبوع. — Āh, bitzūrna kull usbūʿ. — Yes, she visits us every week.
