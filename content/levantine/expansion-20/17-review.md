# 17 · Ask about family

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Checking on a family and arranging a brief visit tomorrow.

Teaching note: Ahlak asks a male listener about his family; ahli refers to the speaker's family. Mnāḥ is a proposed plural form from F12.2, not a claim of speaker-verified pronunciation.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: كيف أهلك اليوم؟ — Kīf ahlak il-yōm? — How is your family today?
- Knafeh: الحمد لله، أهلي مناح. — Il-ḥamdu lillāh, ahli mnāḥ. — Thank God, my family are well.
- Fattoush: وأمك، كيفها؟ — W-immak, kīfha? — And your mother, how is she?
- Knafeh: منيحة، بالبيت اليوم. — Mnīḥa, bil-bēt il-yōm. — She's well, at home today.
- Fattoush: نزورها بكرا؟ — Nzūrha bukra? — Shall we visit her tomorrow?
- Knafeh: آه، بعد الشغل. — Āh, baʿd ish-shughl. — Yes, after work.
