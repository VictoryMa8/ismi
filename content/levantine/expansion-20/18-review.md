Scenario: Catching up after work and adapting the evening to someone who wants a rest.

Teaching note: Kīf kān is a past-time question about the day; raḥ introduces the next action. Compare resting now with a later shared meal, without treating a selection exercise as free conversation.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: كيف كان يومك؟ — Kīf kān yōmak? — How was your day?
- Knafeh: كان طويل، بس منيح. — Kān ṭawīl, bas mnīḥ. — It was long, but good.
- Fattoush: شو عملت بعد الشغل؟ — Shū ʿmilt baʿd ish-shughl? — What did you do after work?
- Knafeh: رحت عالبيت وارتحت شوي. — Ruḥt ʿa-l-bēt w-irtaḥt shway. — I went home and rested a little.
- Fattoush: وهلأ شو رح نعمل؟ — W-hallaʾ shū raḥ niʿmal? — And now, what will we do?
- Knafeh: خلينا ناكل سوا. — Khallīna nākul sawa. — Let's eat together.
