# 19 · Invite someone for a meal

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Inviting a friend for tomorrow rather than tonight and confirming the shared meal.

Teaching note: ʿAnna means at our place. The day and time are part of the invitation: an otherwise natural response with the wrong day does not confirm this arrangement.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: بتيجي عنا بكرا؟ — Btīji ʿanna bukra? — Will you come to our place tomorrow?
- Knafeh: أكيد، أي ساعة؟ — Akīd, ayy sāʿa? — Of course, what time?
- Fattoush: تعال الساعة ستة. — Taʿāl is-sāʿa sitte. — Come at six.
- Knafeh: شو رح ناكل؟ — Shū raḥ nākul? — What will we eat?
- Fattoush: رح نعمل شوربة وسلطة. — Raḥ niʿmal shōraba w-salaṭa. — We'll make soup and salad.
- Knafeh: تمام، بكرا الساعة ستة عندكم. — Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sitte ʿindkum. — Okay, tomorrow at six at your place.
