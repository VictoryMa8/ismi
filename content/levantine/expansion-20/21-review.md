# 21 · Choose somewhere to eat

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Choosing somewhere nearby to eat, asking about the menu and agreeing on a place.

Teaching note: Qarīb and baʿīd contrast distance; a near restaurant is not a direction. The urban q-to-ʾ convention here is pronunciation help requiring review, not one universal Palestinian realization.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: وين ناكل اليوم؟ — Wēn nākul il-yōm? — Where shall we eat today?
- Knafeh: في مطعم صغير قريب من هون. — Fī maṭʿam zghīr ʾarīb min hōn. — There's a small restaurant near here.
- Fattoush: عندهم أكل نباتي؟ — ʿIndhum akil nabāti? — Do they have vegetarian food?
- Knafeh: آه، عندهم حمص وفلافل. — Āh, ʿindhum ḥummuṣ w-falāfil. — Yes, they have hummus and falafel.
- Fattoush: بعيد عن البيت؟ — Bʿīd ʿan il-bēt? — Is it far from home?
- Knafeh: لا، قريب من بيتنا. — Laʾ, ʾarīb min bētna. — No, it's near our home.
