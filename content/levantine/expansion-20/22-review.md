# 22 · Order together

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Agreeing on an order together; each person chooses a meal and they share water.

Teaching note: Biddi marks an individual request while nṭlub is a shared proposal. Keep my order, your order and the shared drink distinct.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: شو بدك تطلب؟ — Shū biddak tiṭlub? — What would you like to order?
- Knafeh: بدي صحن حمص وسلطة. — Biddi ṣaḥn ḥummuṣ w-salaṭa. — I'd like a plate of hummus and a salad.
- Fattoush: وأنا بدي شوربة. — W-ana biddi shōraba. — And I would like soup.
- Knafeh: نطلب مي للإتنين؟ — Niṭlub mayy lil-itnēn? — Shall we order water for both of us?
- Fattoush: آه، قنينة كبيرة. — Āh, ʾnīne kbīre. — Yes, a large bottle.
- Knafeh: تمام، هيك الطلب كامل. — Tamām, hēk iṭ-ṭalab kāmil. — Okay, that completes the order.
