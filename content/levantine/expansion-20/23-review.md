# 23 · Explain a food preference

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Explaining a food preference and checking an ingredient without making medical guarantees.

Teaching note: Bākul states a personal eating practice; fīha asks what is in this dish. This is a language scenario, not an allergy-safety or dietary-certification claim.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: شو بتحب تاكل؟ — Shū bitḥibb tākul? — What do you like to eat?
- Knafeh: بحب الخضرة، وما باكل لحمة. — Baḥibb il-khuḍra, w-mā bākul laḥme. — I like vegetables, and I don't eat meat.
- Fattoush: هاي الشوربة فيها لحمة؟ — Hāy ish-shōraba fīha laḥme? — Does this soup have meat in it?
- Knafeh: آه، فيها دجاج. — Āh, fīha djāj. — Yes, it has chicken.
- Fattoush: خلينا نطلب سلطة بدون لحمة. — Khallīna niṭlub salaṭa bidūn laḥme. — Let's order a salad without meat.
- Knafeh: تمام، وبنسأل عن المكونات. — Tamām, w-bnisʾal ʿan il-mukawwināt. — Okay, and we'll ask about the ingredients.
