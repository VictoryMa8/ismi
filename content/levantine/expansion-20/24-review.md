# 24 · Ask for a change

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Asking to change a drink and its temperature before the order is final.

Teaching note: Badal substitutes one item for another. Bārde/kbīre are feminine agreement with mayy/ʾnīne in these authored phrases. Confirm the changed order explicitly.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: بدك عصير مع الأكل؟ — Biddak ʿaṣīr maʿ il-akil? — Would you like juice with the food?
- Knafeh: ممكن مي بدل العصير؟ — Mumkin mayy badal il-ʿaṣīr? — Could I have water instead of juice?
- Fattoush: أكيد، باردة ولا عادية؟ — Akīd, bārde walla ʿādiyye? — Of course, cold or room temperature?
- Knafeh: عادية، لو سمحتِ. — ʿĀdiyye, law samaḥti. — Room temperature, please.
- Fattoush: وقنينة كبيرة؟ — W-ʾnīne kbīre? — And a large bottle?
- Knafeh: آه، مي عادية بقنينة كبيرة. — Āh, mayy ʿādiyye b-ʾnīne kbīre. — Yes, room-temperature water in a large bottle.
