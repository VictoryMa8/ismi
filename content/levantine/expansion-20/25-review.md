# 25 · Plan a meal for friends

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Planning a meal for four guests and distributing preparation tasks without claiming measured serving sizes.

Teaching note: Arbaʿ ashkhāṣ is a count of people, not a recipe quantity. Baʿdēn sequences tasks; The preparation phrase here is a shared preparation proposal.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: كم شخص رح ييجي؟ — Kam shakhṣ raḥ yīji? — How many people will come?
- Knafeh: أربعة أشخاص، الساعة سبعة. — Arbaʿ ashkhāṣ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. — Four people, at seven.
- Fattoush: نعمل شوربة وسلطة؟ — Niʿmal shōraba w-salaṭa? — Shall we make soup and salad?
- Knafeh: آه، وأنا بجيب خبز. — Āh, w-ana bajīb khubiz. — Yes, and I'll bring bread.
- Fattoush: بنطبخ أول، وبعدين بنجهز الطاولة. — Bnuṭbukh awwal, w-baʿdēn bnjahhiz iṭ-ṭāwle. — We'll cook first, then we'll set the table.
- Knafeh: تمام، خلينا نبلش هلأ. — Tamām, khallīna nballish hallaʾ. — Okay, let's start now.
