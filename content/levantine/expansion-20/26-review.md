# 26 · Ask where a place is

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Locating a bakery relative to a pharmacy and asking whether it is near.

Teaching note: Wēn asks location, janb marks beside and qarīb marks near. No real business location or current directions are asserted in this fictional scene.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: وين المخبز هون؟ — Wēn il-makhbaz hōn? — Where is the bakery here?
- Knafeh: جنب الصيدلية، بآخر الشارع. — Janb iṣ-ṣēdaliyye, b-ākhir ish-shāriʿ. — Beside the pharmacy, at the end of the street.
- Fattoush: قريب من هون؟ — ʾArīb min hōn? — Is it near here?
- Knafeh: آه، قريب كتير. — Āh, ʾarīb ktīr. — Yes, it's very close.
- Fattoush: يعني المخبز جنب الصيدلية؟ — Yaʿni il-makhbaz janb iṣ-ṣēdaliyye? — So the bakery is beside the pharmacy?
- Knafeh: بالضبط، مش جنب المطعم. — Biz-zabṭ, mish janb il-maṭʿam. — Exactly, not beside the restaurant.
