# 27 · Understand a short route

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Checking a short walking route in a fictional neighborhood and repairing a left/right misunderstanding.

Teaching note: Dughri means straight ahead here, not a distance. Yamīn and shmāl contrast the turn. Māshi remains a proposal, not proof of listening comprehension.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: كيف نوصل عالمخبز؟ — Kīf nōṣal ʿa-l-makhbaz? — How do we get to the bakery?
- Knafeh: بنمشي دغري، وبعدين يمين. — Bnimshi dughri, w-baʿdēn yamīn. — We walk straight, then right.
- Fattoush: يمين بعد الصيدلية؟ — Yamīn baʿd iṣ-ṣēdaliyye? — Right after the pharmacy?
- Knafeh: آه، مش شمال. — Āh, mish shmāl. — Yes, not left.
- Fattoush: وبعدين بنشوف المخبز؟ — W-baʿdēn bnshūf il-makhbaz? — And then we'll see the bakery?
- Knafeh: آه، عاليمين. — Āh, ʿa-l-yamīn. — Yes, on the right.
