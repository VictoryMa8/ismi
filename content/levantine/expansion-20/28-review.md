Scenario: Confirming an exact meeting point and distinguishing inside from outside.

Teaching note: Quddām names in front of. Juwwa and barra contrast inside/outside. The dialogue checks both which place and which side of its entrance.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: وين نلتقي؟ — Wēn niltaʾi? — Where shall we meet?
- Knafeh: قدام المكتبة، الساعة خمسة. — ʾUddām il-maktabe, is-sāʿa khamse. — In front of the library, at five.
- Fattoush: جوا المكتبة، ولا برا؟ — Juwwa il-maktabe, walla barra? — Inside the library, or outside?
- Knafeh: برا، جنب الباب. — Barra, janb il-bāb. — Outside, beside the door.
- Fattoush: باب المكتبة، مش باب المقهى؟ — Bāb il-maktabe, mish bāb il-maʾha? — The library door, not the cafe door?
- Knafeh: آه، عند باب المكتبة. — Āh, ʿind bāb il-maktabe. — Yes, at the library door.
