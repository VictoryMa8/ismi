# 29 · Explain where you are

**Unreviewed, text-only development preview.** No human linguistic/source acceptance or human audio.

Scenario: Telling someone where you are now and correcting the wrong street.

Teaching note: Hallaʾ marks now. Lissa means still/not yet depending on context; lissa barra here is still outside. The street distinction is fictional, not navigational advice.

## Dialogue

- Fattoush: وينك هلأ؟ — Wēnak hallaʾ? — Where are you now?
- Knafeh: أنا عند الصيدلية، جنب البنك. — Ana ʿind iṣ-ṣēdaliyye, janb il-bank. — I'm at the pharmacy, beside the bank.
- Fattoush: بالشارع الكبير، صح؟ — Bish-shāriʿ il-kbīr, ṣaḥḥ? — On the big street, right?
- Knafeh: لا، بالشارع الصغير. — Laʾ, bish-shāriʿ iṣ-ṣghīr. — No, on the small street.
- Fattoush: دخلت عالصيدلية؟ — Dakhalt ʿa-ṣ-ṣēdaliyye? — Did you go into the pharmacy?
- Knafeh: لا، لسا برا. — Laʾ, lissa barra. — No, I'm still outside.
