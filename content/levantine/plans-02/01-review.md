# Choose a plan

**Draft for owner review — October 4, 2026.** No publication or expert review.

Propose a meal, ask for a time, and distinguish a meal plan from a tea plan.

Fattoush and Knafeh are planning tomorrow evening. They choose a meal at seven, with tea afterwards. Later practice asks you to adapt when the requested activity changes.

## Dialogue

**Fattoush**: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟  
Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

**Knafeh**: نعمل رز وسلطة؟  
Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? — Shall we make rice and salad?

**Fattoush**: تمام. أي ساعة؟  
Tamām. Ayy sāʿa? — Okay. What time?

**Knafeh**: الساعة سبعة.  
Is-sāʿa sabʿa. — At seven o’clock.

**Fattoush**: نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة.  
Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa. — We’ll make rice and salad at seven.

**Knafeh**: وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا.  
W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. — And afterwards we’ll drink tea together.

## Teaching and recall

### Propose a meal

Suggest making rice and salad.

نعمل رز وسلطة؟ — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa?
Shall we make rice and salad?

Niʿmal can propose a shared activity in a question. W- joins both foods.

### Ask for the missing detail

The meal is agreed, but no time is set. Ask for it.

تمام. أي ساعة؟ — Tamām. Ayy sāʿa?
Okay. What time?

Accept the activity with tamām, then ask ayy sāʿa when the time is missing.

### Confirm the meal and time

Confirm rice and salad at seven.

نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة. — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa.
We’ll make rice and salad at seven.

Combine the familiar meal with is-sāʿa sabʿa. Tomorrow is already established.

### Add what happens next

Add tea together after the meal.

وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا. — W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa.
And afterwards we’ll drink tea together.

Baʿdēn links tea to the meal already discussed; it does not give a clock time.

### Adapt the activity and time

Make a different plan: tea together tomorrow at six.

بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. — Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa.
Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together.

Reuse the day/time frame with nishrab shāy sawa. This new arrangement is tea at six.

## Contextual practice

### p01-01

As Knafeh, suggest making rice and salad for tomorrow.

Prompt: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ — Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

- **a (accepted):** نعمل رز وسلطة؟ / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? / Shall we make rice and salad? — This proposes the requested meal.
- **b:** نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟ / Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl? / Shall we visit my family after work? — A family visit is possible, but the requested plan is a meal.
- **c:** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — This proposes tea rather than the requested meal.

Retry hint: Name both foods.

### p01-02

The meal sounds good, but Knafeh has not given a time. Accept and ask when.

Prompt: نعمل رز وسلطة؟ — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? — Shall we make rice and salad?

- **a:** وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا. / W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. / And afterwards we’ll drink tea together. — This adds an activity but leaves the time unanswered.
- **b (accepted):** تمام. أي ساعة؟ / Tamām. Ayy sāʿa? / Okay. What time? — This accepts the meal and asks for the missing time.
- **c:** الساعة سبعة. / Is-sāʿa sabʿa. / At seven o’clock. — This supplies a time rather than asking for one.

Retry hint: Ask rather than guess.

### p01-03

Knafeh says seven. Confirm the rice-and-salad plan at that time.

Prompt: الساعة سبعة. — Is-sāʿa sabʿa. — At seven o’clock.

- **a:** نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة ستة. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sitte. / We’ll make rice and salad at six. — The meal fits, but six changes the agreed time.
- **b:** آه، بكرا الساعة سبعة نشرب شاي سوا. / Āh, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nishrab shāy sawa. / Yes, tomorrow at seven we’ll drink tea together. — Seven fits, but tea replaces the meal.
- **c (accepted):** نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa. / We’ll make rice and salad at seven. — Both the meal and seven match the arrangement.

Retry hint: Preserve both activity and time.

### p01-04

The meal at seven is agreed. As Knafeh, suggest drinking tea together afterwards.

Prompt: نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة. — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa. — We’ll make rice and salad at seven.

- **a (accepted):** وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا. / W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. / And afterwards we’ll drink tea together. — This adds shared tea after the agreed meal.
- **b:** وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. / W-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. / And afterwards we’ll make rice and salad. — This proposes another meal instead of tea.
- **c:** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — This moves tea to six rather than adding it afterwards.

Retry hint: Use the next-event link.

### p01-05

New arrangement: Fattoush asks what you will do tomorrow. Propose only tea together at six.

Prompt: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ — Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

- **a:** نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة ستة. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sitte. / We’ll make rice and salad at six. — The day can be understood, but this changes tea into a meal.
- **b (accepted):** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — This gives the requested day, time and shared drink.
- **c:** اليوم الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Il-yōm is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Today at six we’ll drink tea together. — The activity and time fit, but this changes tomorrow to today.

Retry hint: Keep tomorrow, six and tea.

### p01-06

New arrangement: agree to tea together tomorrow at seven.

Prompt: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ — Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

- **a:** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — This changes seven to six.
- **b:** نعمل رز وسلطة الساعة سبعة. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa is-sāʿa sabʿa. / We’ll make rice and salad at seven. — This changes tea into rice and salad.
- **c (accepted):** آه، بكرا الساعة سبعة نشرب شاي سوا. / Āh, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nishrab shāy sawa. / Yes, tomorrow at seven we’ll drink tea together. — This agrees to the requested tea plan at seven tomorrow.

Retry hint: Keep the new details, not the earlier six-o’clock plan.
