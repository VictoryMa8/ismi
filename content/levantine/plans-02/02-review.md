# What happens first?

**Draft for owner review — October 4, 2026.** No publication or expert review.

Link two shared activities in order, and ask for a clock time when “afterwards” is not precise enough.

Tomorrow evening, Fattoush and Knafeh agree to tea at six and a meal afterwards. In practice, a new instruction may reverse the order: preserve the order requested in that exchange.

## Dialogue

**Fattoush**: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟  
Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

**Knafeh**: نشرب شاي وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة.  
Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. — We’ll drink tea and then make rice and salad.

**Fattoush**: تمام. أي ساعة؟  
Tamām. Ayy sāʿa? — Okay. What time?

**Knafeh**: بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا.  
Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. — Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together.

**Fattoush**: وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة.  
W-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. — And afterwards we’ll make rice and salad.

**Knafeh**: آه، خلّينا نعمل رز وسلطة.  
Āh, khallīna niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. — Yes, let’s make rice and salad.

## Teaching and recall

### Tea first, meal second

Propose tea, then making rice and salad.

نشرب شاي وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. — Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa.
We’ll drink tea and then make rice and salad.

The activity before w-baʿdēn happens first. Here that is tea.

### Reverse the order deliberately

The plan changes: make the meal first, then drink tea.

نعمل رز وسلطة وبعدين نشرب شاي. — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy.
We’ll make rice and salad and then drink tea.

Use the same two activities with a new order: meal before tea.

### Ask for clock time

Tea comes first, but when? Ask for the time.

تمام. أي ساعة؟ — Tamām. Ayy sāʿa?
Okay. What time?

A sequence alone does not say when to start. Ayy sāʿa asks for that detail.

### Give the first activity a time

Confirm tea together tomorrow at six.

بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. — Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa.
Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together.

This puts tea at six tomorrow. It does not set an exact time for the later meal.

### Keep the second activity connected

Tea is agreed. Add making rice and salad afterwards.

وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. — W-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa.
And afterwards we’ll make rice and salad.

After tea is established, w-baʿdēn connects the meal to it.

## Contextual practice

### p02-01

Propose tea first and making rice and salad afterwards.

Prompt: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ — Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

- **a (accepted):** نشرب شاي وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. / Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. / We’ll drink tea and then make rice and salad. — Tea precedes the meal, as requested.
- **b:** نعمل رز وسلطة وبعدين نشرب شاي. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy. / We’ll make rice and salad and then drink tea. — Both activities fit, but their order is reversed.
- **c:** نزور أهلك وبعدين نشرب شاي. / Nzūr ahlak w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy. / We’ll visit your family and then drink tea. (to Knafeh) — This starts with a family visit, not the requested tea and meal.

Retry hint: Start with tea.

### p02-02

You understand the activity order, but still need a clock time. Ask for it.

Prompt: نشرب شاي وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. — Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. — We’ll drink tea and then make rice and salad.

- **a:** الساعة ستة. / Is-sāʿa sitte. / At six o’clock. — This gives a time rather than asking.
- **b (accepted):** تمام. أي ساعة؟ / Tamām. Ayy sāʿa? / Okay. What time? — This asks for the missing clock time.
- **c:** وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. / W-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. / And afterwards we’ll make rice and salad. — This repeats the sequence without asking for a time.

Retry hint: Ask ayy sāʿa.

### p02-03

Give the start of this arrangement: tomorrow at six, drink tea together.

Prompt: تمام. أي ساعة؟ — Tamām. Ayy sāʿa? — Okay. What time?

- **a:** آه، بكرا الساعة سبعة نشرب شاي سوا. / Āh, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nishrab shāy sawa. / Yes, tomorrow at seven we’ll drink tea together. — The activity is right but seven is not six.
- **b:** اليوم الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Il-yōm is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Today at six we’ll drink tea together. — This changes the agreed day to today.
- **c (accepted):** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — This names the starting activity, day and time.

Retry hint: Tomorrow and six both matter.

### p02-04

Tea at six is now agreed. Add the meal as the next activity.

Prompt: بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. — Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. — Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together.

- **a (accepted):** وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. / W-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. / And afterwards we’ll make rice and salad. — This adds the meal after tea.
- **b:** وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا. / W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. / And afterwards we’ll drink tea together. — This repeats tea as the next activity instead of adding the meal.
- **c:** نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟ / Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl? / Shall we visit my family after work? — This introduces a visit instead of the meal.

Retry hint: Link the meal with afterwards.

### p02-05

New plan: Knafeh suggests the meal. As Fattoush, confirm making rice and salad FIRST, then drinking tea.

Prompt: نعمل رز وسلطة؟ — Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa? — Shall we make rice and salad?

- **a:** وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا. / W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. / And afterwards we’ll drink tea together. — This adds tea but does not explicitly confirm both activities as requested.
- **b (accepted):** نعمل رز وسلطة وبعدين نشرب شاي. / Niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy. / We’ll make rice and salad and then drink tea. — This confirms the meal first and tea afterwards.
- **c:** نشرب شاي وبعدين نعمل رز وسلطة. / Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn niʿmal ruzz w-salaṭa. / We’ll drink tea and then make rice and salad. — This reverses the newly requested order.

Retry hint: State both activities in the new order.

### p02-06

Transfer: Knafeh suggests visiting his family after work. Confirm visiting HIS family, then drinking tea.

Prompt: نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟ — Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl? — Shall we visit my family after work?

- **a:** نشرب شاي وبعدين نزور أهلك. / Nishrab shāy w-baʿdēn nzūr ahlak. / We’ll drink tea and then visit your family. (to Knafeh) — This reverses the visit and tea.
- **b:** بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلي. / Bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahli. / Tomorrow at seven we’ll visit my family. — Ahli would refer to your own family; it also invents a time.
- **c (accepted):** نزور أهلك وبعدين نشرب شاي. / Nzūr ahlak w-baʿdēn nishrab shāy. / We’ll visit your family and then drink tea. (to Knafeh) — Ahlak refers to Knafeh’s family, and tea comes afterwards.

Retry hint: Whose family, and what comes next?
