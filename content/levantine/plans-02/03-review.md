# Clear up the plan

**Draft for owner review — October 4, 2026.** No publication or expert review.

Correct a misunderstood time or day and confirm whose family you will visit.

Knafeh and Fattoush arrange a visit to Knafeh’s family tomorrow at seven in the evening. Fattoush initially hears six. Use brief corrections and a final confirmation; later practice changes the details.

## Dialogue

**Knafeh**: بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلي.  
Bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahli. — Tomorrow at seven we’ll visit my family.

**Fattoush**: الساعة ستة؟  
Is-sāʿa sitte? — At six o’clock?

**Knafeh**: لا، الساعة سبعة.  
Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. — No, at seven o’clock.

**Fattoush**: تمام، بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلك.  
Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahlak. — Okay, tomorrow at seven we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh)

**Knafeh**: وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا.  
W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa. — And afterwards we’ll drink tea together.

**Fattoush**: بدي شاي.  
Biddi shāy. — I want tea.

## Teaching and recall

### Check what you heard

You think you heard six. Check that time.

الساعة ستة؟ — Is-sāʿa sitte?
At six o’clock?

The question repeats the time to check it. Its intonation differs from a statement; no reviewed audio is available yet.

### Repair the time

Fattoush heard six, but you meant seven. Correct the time.

لا، الساعة سبعة. — Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa.
No, at seven o’clock.

Laʾ rejects the repeated six; is-sāʿa sabʿa gives the intended time.

### Repair the day

Someone checks “today?” You mean tomorrow. Correct them.

لا، بكرا. — Laʾ, bukra.
No, tomorrow.

When the question is about today, laʾ, bukra corrects only the day.

### Confirm from the listener’s perspective

Confirm to Knafeh: tomorrow at seven, visit his family.

تمام، بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلك. — Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahlak.
Okay, tomorrow at seven we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh)

Knafeh says ahli, my family. Fattoush says ahlak, your family, to refer to those same people.

### Use the actual new time

New plan: the intended time is six, not seven. Correct the time.

لا، الساعة ستة. — Laʾ, is-sāʿa sitte.
No, at six o’clock.

Do not memorize seven as the right answer. A different arrangement can require six.

## Contextual practice

### p03-01

Fattoush heard six. As Knafeh, correct her: the visit is at seven.

Prompt: الساعة ستة؟ — Is-sāʿa sitte? — At six o’clock?

- **a (accepted):** لا، الساعة سبعة. / Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. / No, at seven o’clock. — This directly corrects six to seven.
- **b:** لا، الساعة ستة. / Laʾ, is-sāʿa sitte. / No, at six o’clock. — This repeats the mistaken six instead of correcting it.
- **c:** لا، بكرا. / Laʾ, bukra. / No, tomorrow. — Tomorrow may fit, but it does not resolve the mistaken time.

Retry hint: Correct the hour.

### p03-02

Knafeh corrected the time. Confirm tomorrow at seven and visiting HIS family.

Prompt: لا، الساعة سبعة. — Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. — No, at seven o’clock.

- **a:** بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلي. / Bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahli. / Tomorrow at seven we’ll visit my family. — As Fattoush, ahli would change the visit to her own family.
- **b (accepted):** تمام، بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلك. / Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahlak. / Okay, tomorrow at seven we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh) — This preserves tomorrow and seven; ahlak refers to Knafeh’s family.
- **c:** تمام، بكرا الساعة ستة نزور أهلك. / Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sitte nzūr ahlak. / Okay, tomorrow at six we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh) — This restores the mistaken time of six.

Retry hint: Keep seven and your listener’s family.

### p03-03

New misunderstanding: Fattoush checks “today?” You mean tomorrow. Correct only the day.

Prompt: اليوم؟ — Il-yōm? — Today?

- **a:** لا، الساعة سبعة. / Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. / No, at seven o’clock. — This supplies seven but leaves the day unclear.
- **b:** اليوم الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Il-yōm is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Today at six we’ll drink tea together. — This confirms the mistaken today and invents a time.
- **c (accepted):** لا، بكرا. / Laʾ, bukra. / No, tomorrow. — This replaces today with tomorrow.

Retry hint: Give the right day.

### p03-04

Transfer: the agreed visit is now at SIX tomorrow. As Fattoush, correct Knafeh when he says seven.

Prompt: الساعة سبعة. — Is-sāʿa sabʿa. — At seven o’clock.

- **a (accepted):** لا، الساعة ستة. / Laʾ, is-sāʿa sitte. / No, at six o’clock. — This corrects the time to the new intended six.
- **b:** لا، الساعة سبعة. / Laʾ, is-sāʿa sabʿa. / No, at seven o’clock. — This keeps seven, which is wrong for this new arrangement.
- **c:** لا، بكرا. / Laʾ, bukra. / No, tomorrow. — Tomorrow is already agreed; the mistake is the time.

Retry hint: The new plan is six.

### p03-05

Continue the new six-o’clock arrangement. Knafeh says “my family.” Confirm tomorrow at six and HIS family.

Prompt: نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟ — Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl? — Shall we visit my family after work?

- **a:** بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلي. / Bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahli. / Tomorrow at seven we’ll visit my family. — This changes both the time and whose family will be visited.
- **b (accepted):** تمام، بكرا الساعة ستة نزور أهلك. / Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sitte nzūr ahlak. / Okay, tomorrow at six we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh) — This preserves the new six-o’clock time and refers to Knafeh’s family.
- **c:** تمام، بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلك. / Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahlak. / Okay, tomorrow at seven we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh) — This carries over seven from the old plan.

Retry hint: Confirm six and ahlak.

### p03-06

New activity: Fattoush asks about tomorrow. Agree to tea together at seven, with no family visit in this plan.

Prompt: شو رح نعمل بكرا؟ — Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra? — What will we do tomorrow?

- **a:** بكرا الساعة سبعة نزور أهلي. / Bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nzūr ahli. / Tomorrow at seven we’ll visit my family. — Seven fits, but the family visit is not part of this arrangement.
- **b:** بكرا الساعة ستة نشرب شاي سوا. / Bukra is-sāʿa sitte nishrab shāy sawa. / Tomorrow at six we’ll drink tea together. — Tea fits, but six changes the requested time.
- **c (accepted):** آه، بكرا الساعة سبعة نشرب شاي سوا. / Āh, bukra is-sāʿa sabʿa nishrab shāy sawa. / Yes, tomorrow at seven we’ll drink tea together. — This confirms exactly the new day, time and activity.

Retry hint: Check all three: day, time, activity.

## Review boundaries

A short laʾ plus the corrected detail can repair a misunderstanding. Keep the correction about the mistaken detail, then confirm the full plan. These are bounded exchanges, not a universal politeness rule.
