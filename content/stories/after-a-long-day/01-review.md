## A quick hello

Fattoush hopes to catch up with Knafeh. First, she checks how he is doing.

**Fattoush** — شو عامل؟

Shū ʿāmel?

How are you doing? (casual check-in to Knafeh)

**Knafeh** — أنا منيح. وإنتِ؟

Ana mnīḥ. W-inti?

I’m well. And you? (to Fattoush)

## Both need a pause

The conversation reveals a small problem: neither has much energy for a long catch-up.

**Fattoush** — أنا تعبانة شوي.

Ana taʿbāne shwayy.

I’m a little tired. (Fattoush speaking)

**Knafeh** — أنا تعبان كمان.

Ana taʿbān kamān.

I’m tired too. (Knafeh speaking)

## A little rest

Fattoush offers a pause. Knafeh says what he needs; their catch-up can wait.

**Fattoush** — بدك ترتاح؟

Biddak tirtāḥ?

Do you want to rest? (to Knafeh)

**Knafeh** — آه، بدي أرتاح شوي.

Āh, biddi artāḥ shwayy.

Yes, I want to rest a little.
