## Ready for a drink

After their short rest, they have enough energy for a quiet catch-up. Knafeh offers Fattoush a choice of drinks.

**Knafeh** — بدك شاي ولا قهوة؟

Biddik shāy walla ʾahwe?

Do you want tea or coffee? (to Fattoush)

**Fattoush** — شاي، لو سمحت.

Shāy, law samaḥt.

Tea, please. (to Knafeh)

## One small detail

The drink is chosen, but Knafeh still needs to know whether Fattoush wants sugar. Her answer settles that detail.

**Knafeh** — بدك سكر؟

Biddik sukkar?

Do you want sugar?

**Fattoush** — لا، بدون سكر، شكراً.

Laʾ, bidūn sukkar, shukran.

No, without sugar, thank you.

## Different favorites

They do not have to choose the same drink to enjoy the break together. Knafeh says what he wants, and Fattoush explains her preference.

**Knafeh** — أنا بدي قهوة.

Ana biddi ʾahwe.

I want coffee.

**Fattoush** — بحب الشاي أكتر.

Baḥibb ish-shāy aktar.

I like tea more.

## Comprehension check
