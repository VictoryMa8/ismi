## Before the break ends

Their rest and tea have made room for a short conversation. Before ending the catch-up, Fattoush asks about tomorrow. Knafeh suggests a visit after work.

**Fattoush** — شو رح نعمل بكرا؟

Shū raḥ niʿmal bukra?

What will we do tomorrow?

**Knafeh** — نزور أهلي بعد الشغل؟

Nzūr ahli baʿd ish-shughl?

Shall we visit my family after work?

## A missing time

The idea sounds good, but after work does not give a clock time. Fattoush asks for the detail they still need.

**Fattoush** — تمام. أي ساعة؟

Tamām. Ayy sāʿa?

Okay. What time?

**Knafeh** — الساعة ستة.

Is-sāʿa sitte.

At six o’clock.

## The same plan

Fattoush confirms the day, time and whose family they will visit. Knafeh adds tea together afterwards, giving them another chance to catch up.

**Fattoush** — تمام، بكرا الساعة ستة نزور أهلك.

Tamām, bukra is-sāʿa sitte nzūr ahlak.

Okay, tomorrow at six we’ll visit your family. (to Knafeh)

**Knafeh** — وبعدين نشرب شاي سوا.

W-baʿdēn nishrab shāy sawa.

And afterwards we’ll drink tea together.

## Comprehension check

Whose family do they plan to visit tomorrow?

Taught target: `e06-02` · source index `0`.

- **Alternative:** Fattoush’s family.
  Rationale: Knafeh says my family. When Fattoush confirms to him, she says your family.

- **Accepted:** Knafeh’s family.
  Rationale: Knafeh proposes visiting his own family; Fattoush confirms that same family from her perspective.

- **Alternative:** Neither family; they only plan tea.
  Rationale: Tea comes afterwards. The family visit is part of the agreed plan.

Explanation: Ahli is my family when Knafeh speaks. Ahlak in Fattoush’s confirmation refers back to his family.

## Comprehension check

When do they agree to visit?

Taught target: `e06-04` · source index `0`.

- **Accepted:** Tomorrow at six, after work.
  Rationale: Knafeh suggests after work and gives six; Fattoush confirms tomorrow at six.

- **Alternative:** Today at six, after work.
  Rationale: Six is correct, but bukra places the plan tomorrow rather than today.

- **Alternative:** Tomorrow at seven, after work.
  Rationale: Tomorrow is correct, but the scene agrees on six, not seven.

Explanation: The final confirmation brings together tomorrow, six o’clock and the family visit after work.
