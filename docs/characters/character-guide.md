# Lina and Omar

October 1, 2026. Implementation proposal, incorporating the owner's direction:
“cartoonish, like kind of corporate artish.” Final character selection and
curriculum publication remain owner decisions.

The [concept sheet](corporate-cartoon-concept-v1.png) uses flat geometric faces,
simple eyes and clear silhouettes. Lina has dark wavy hair and a sage shirt;
Omar has short curly hair, a graphic beard and a terracotta shirt. Both are
adults. These are fictional visual designs, not regional appearance standards.
No city, occupation, religion, family relationship or romance is assigned.

Lina is warm, observant and direct; Omar is thoughtful and easygoing. These are
editorial notes for later approved stories. This implementation adds no new
biography, Arabic, preferences or dialogue. Their relationship remains the
existing scenario's two people who know each other.

The sheet shows each character's neutral front, attentive three-quarter,
encouraging front and profile views. Hair, face, outfit and proportions remain
consistent across views. Use neutral portraits for recall and response roles,
attentive portraits for authored speakers, and the same gentle encouragement
after either a correct or incorrect attempt. Never signal an expected answer,
disappointment, a reward, or a grammatical form through art.

Images are decorative. Names and textual roles explain the exchange. Each
portrait occupies a fixed square; unavailable images use a decorative initial
and retain the written label. Generic grammar/topic cues stay unassigned.
There are no character animations, delays, voice assignments or account-gender
changes. Real recording contributor credits remain independent.

The bundled `ismi-cast-v1` registry resolves `lina` and `omar`; its WebP is
precached with the PWA shell. Preserve this asset path for supported packages.
A different future design should get a new registry/path, rather than replacing
the meaning of an existing version. The [asset record](asset-provenance.json)
includes authorship, history, hashes, delivery processing and usage notes;
the [exact prompt](generation-prompt.txt) records the built-in image generation.

Preview the metadata proposals in the owner console. Inspect scene, hidden
recall, contextual response and mistake feedback at mobile and desktop sizes.
Saved [desktop](lesson-1-desktop-preview.png) and
[mobile](lesson-1-mobile-preview.png) screenshots show the disposable test preview.
Choose or revise the designs before owner publication. The portrait layer does
not establish expert review, accessibility release readiness or learning efficacy.
