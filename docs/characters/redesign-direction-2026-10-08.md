# Character redesign direction

October 8, 2026 · F06.1 · Initial recommendation, followed by owner-authorized kickoff.

The owner reports that Fattoush and Knafeh still look AI generated. The current
sheet is explicitly AI generated ([provenance](asset-provenance.json)); the exact
v1 art was approved for the October 3 release. That release remains historical
evidence, not approval of a future redesign.

Visual inspection of [the current sheet](corporate-cartoon-concept-v1.png) shows
soft shading, similar facial constructions, and relatively subtle differences
between neutral and encouraging expressions. These are design observations, not
a claim that an image's authorship can be reliably detected from its appearance.

## Recommended workflow

Use deliberately drawn, editable vector characters. First compare rough silhouettes
and face sketches at actual mobile portrait size. Approve one design per character,
then establish a model sheet with fixed head/body proportions, palette, hair shapes,
line weights and expressions. Build the existing neutral, attentive, encouraging
and profile views from that design. Keep the characters warm, adult and distinctive,
without assigning cultural stereotypes or unapproved biographies.

A human illustrator is the strongest route for intentional art direction; a custom,
simple SVG design assembled from reusable paths is the lower-cost alternative.
A human can still refine AI concepts, but another generated raster sheet alone is
unlikely to address this owner's concern. This is an editorial recommendation,
not a guarantee about perceived authorship or a decision to commission work.

Vector objects can be edited, grouped and transformed precisely, supporting this
reuse workflow ([Adobe's editing guide](https://www.adobe.com/learn/illustrator/web/edit-artwork-basics)).
Shared vector graphics and palettes can also be reused across files
([Adobe's asset-library documentation](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/using/creative-cloud-libraries-sync-share-assets.html)).
No particular paid software is required by this proposal.

## Acceptance and delivery

Owner approves the concept and model sheet before replacement. Record actual
creator, editable source, usage rights and exported hashes. Compare all expressions
at mobile/desktop sizes and verify stable square framing, decorative accessibility,
written names/roles, failure fallback and offline rendering. Preserve `ismi-cast-v1`
assets for supported packages; give new artwork a new registry/path. Existing neutral
and encouragement behavior must not reveal answers or shame learners.

The owner subsequently authorized the redesign kickoff. An [editable vector concept
and portrait preview](vector-concept-02/README.md) are ready for feedback. No current
app characters were replaced, commissioned, published or deployed.


## Owner rejection of the SVG method

Both local SVG studies were rejected. The owner explicitly scrapped this method;
retain the studies only as historical comparisons, not pending design candidates.
Do not continue programmatic SVG-path illustration or integrate these assets.
This rejection is about the creation method and resulting quality, not a restriction
on a professional illustrator delivering vector files.

Recommended next route: select a character illustrator from finished portfolio
work, then agree a bounded concept brief for Fattoush and Knafeh before ordering
expressions or turnaround views. [Behance illustrator portfolios](https://www.behance.net/hire/illustrators)
and [Dribbble character designers](https://dribbble.com/designers/character-design)
provide discovery options; no individual artist, quote or availability is verified.
Licensed existing illustrations trade uniqueness for ready-made consistency.
Reference-led image-generation concepts remain another option, without a guarantee
of human-illustrated appearance or angle consistency. Hiring/contact/spending and
asset purchase are not authorized by this recommendation request.
