## Direction

Fattoush: broad bob silhouette, angular gold collar, green shirt, gold earrings.
Knafeh: compact curls, long bearded face, red shirt and gold neckline.
Charcoal outlines and solid fills replace the existing raster sheet's shading.
Identity geometry stays fixed across neutral, attentive and encouraging faces;
profile supplies a separate angle study. The portraits remain adult, with no new
biography or regional-appearance claim.

This first concept favors compact, readable busts. The attentive view is front-facing
with a speaking mouth; it is not a finished three-quarter turnaround. Owner feedback
on silhouette, facial style, outline weight and expression is the next design step.
Approval of this concept does not imply approval of final exports or app integration.
