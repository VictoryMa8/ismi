## Course design

Target English-speaking adult false beginners who want to speak with a Levantine
partner, family member or friend. Urban Palestinian remains the primary model;
include Jordanian alternatives only with explicit reviewed labels and provenance.
Fattoush and Knafeh recur with authored speaker/addressee mappings. English help and
transliteration stay available. New compositions need exact acceptance even when
all their vocabulary came from an already approved lesson.

The [machine-readable authoring plan](../i-web/src/content/levantine-course-plan.json)
is the single sequence used by the console. Sixty lesson slots are an editable
**authoring budget**, not sixty authored lessons or a new release promise. Preserve
the existing seven Everyday IDs/orders and three Plans IDs/orders. Their two units
occupy planning weeks one/two; this does not establish two measured weeks of study.
New weeks have five scenario lessons each. Titles and English task outlines do not
supply approved Arabic or authorize importing empty curriculum placeholders.

| Week | Communicative focus | Language and retrieval | Evidence task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Everyday conversations | Check-ins, day, family, food, plans | Establish baseline and identify unsupported forms |
| 2 | Make a plan together | Propose, sequence, clarify and confirm | Review three exact existing drafts and repair perspective errors |
| 3 | At home together | Requests, location, needs and negation | Adapt a household exchange to a different request |
| 4 | People and plans | Recombine weeks 1–3 | Five-minute guided month-one exchange |
| 5 | Share a meal | Quantities, preferences and polite requests | Order/adapt a meal in a new context |
| 6 | Around the neighborhood | Location, directions and clarification | Confirm a route and meeting point |
| 7 | Visit and welcome | Offers, acceptance/decline and social questions | Host a short visit with a preference change |
| 8 | Tell me about your week | Bounded recent-event language and follow-ups | Guided cumulative conversation plus delayed recall |
| 9 | Choose things together | Prices, amounts, comparison and reasons | Make and explain a shared choice |
| 10 | A day out | Transport, time and changed arrangements | Repair a travel plan after a delay |
| 11 | Care and connection | Feelings, needs, suggestions and preferences | Offer everyday help and respect a refusal |
| 12 | Keep the conversation going | Cumulative retrieval and clarification | Guided cumulative conversation with unseen details |

Plan for fifteen minutes **devoted to Levantine** per day: one or two five-to-seven
minute micro-lessons as appropriate, plus due review and retrieval in varied contexts.
Use non-lesson days for delayed recall, dialogue practice and catch-up. Never equate
five lesson files with a verified week of instruction. Measure timing with learners;
revise pacing instead of padding with recognition drills. Parallel-language budgets
do not retain each course's fifteen-minute outcome promise automatically.

## F12.7 · Arabic word-preserving character migration

The old compatibility adapter matched the Arabic legacy name as a raw substring,
including inside ordinary words such as خلينا. The API also applied this to escaped
JSON bytes. New packages therefore changed during a save/read cycle and no longer
matched the exact preview fingerprint. Fix both adapters to match complete names.
The API decodes JSON string tokens before word matching and reserializes strings;
literal escaped text retains its meaning. The browser uses Unicode letter/mark/
number boundaries. Original immutable publication snapshots remain untouched.

Done when ordinary Arabic words survive plain/escaped JSON and browser adaptation,
standalone legacy names still migrate, and all twenty packages round-trip and
qualify only for their exact pinned preview exception. Tests cover the reproduced
failure, literal escapes, prior legacy fixtures and complete preview lifecycle.
